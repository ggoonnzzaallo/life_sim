ECOSYSTEM GUIDE

Rules

Movement

Green plants stay still. Yellow herbivores flee nearby predators first, then seek eligible mates, then plants. Blue predators scan over long distances for yellows, but wander slowly when no prey is visible and temporarily slow after eating. Plants remain solid barriers to predators.

Feeding

Herbivores eat plants except for maximum-level perpetual plants. After eating, they slow down and must finish a digestion cooldown before eating again. Predators can eat herbivores of equal displayed size or one pixel larger. Predators never eat one another.

Reproduction

Plants spawn naturally, especially near mature plants. Herbivores must reach level 2 before mating. Eligible pairs produce three offspring when they collide with enough energy. Predators produce one offspring after eating three herbivores. Their offspring spawn at a random location for safety.

Leveling

Plants level up by surviving. Higher levels attract more nearby growth, and a plant that reaches the maximum level becomes perpetual and cannot be eaten. Herbivores and predators level up by eating; higher levels make them larger, faster, and better at seeing food.

Survival

Herbivores and predators steadily lose energy and eventually die without food. All populations share the world population limit.
