ECOSYSTEM GUIDE

Rules

Adjustable marks rules you can tune in Settings.

Movement & instincts

  • Green plants stay still and cannot overlap.
  • Yellow herbivores cannot overlap. They flee predators, seek mates, then seek edible plants.
  • Herds notice predators from farther away. Adjustable
  • Blue predators chase yellows and eligible smaller blues, but wander slowly when no prey is visible. Adjustable
  • Plants are solid barriers to predators. Adjustable

Feeding

  • Herbivores eat plants at their level or below. A maximum-level herbivore can eat a maximum-level plant.
  • Herbivores slow down and must digest between meals. Adjustable
  • Predators can eat herbivores of equal displayed size or slightly larger; apex predators can eat any herbivore. Adjustable
  • Predators slow down and stop hunting while digesting. Adjustable
  • Predators can eat lower-level predators, but level-1 offspring are protected. Adjustable

Reproduction

  • Plants spawn in open ground; mature plants seed more quickly nearby. Adjustable
  • Herbivores must reach a minimum level before mating. Litter size grows with the parents’ energy. Adjustable
  • Predators produce one offspring after enough yellow meals. Adjustable

Migration

  • Herbivores occasionally enter together as a pack through one map edge. Adjustable
  • Predators arrive separately, and migrate more often when fewer than three remain. Adjustable

Leveling

  • Plants level up by surviving, with randomized timing. Adjustable
  • Only one maximum-level plant may exist within a local exclusion radius. Adjustable
  • Herbivores level up by eating plants. Adjustable
  • Predators level up at cumulative prey-meal milestones. Adjustable
  • Higher levels increase size and movement speed. Adjustable

Survival

  • Herbivores and predators lose energy and die without food. Predator energy use and meal energy are adjustable. Adjustable
  • Old-age deaths are off by default and can be enabled. Adjustable
  • All species share the world population limit. Starting populations, map size, and the limit are adjustable. Adjustable
