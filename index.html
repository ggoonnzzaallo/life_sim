ECOSYSTEM GUIDE

Rules

Movement

Green plants stay still and cannot overlap one another. Yellow herbivores cannot overlap; they flee nearby predators first, then seek eligible mates, then plants. Nearby herd members increase their predator-awareness range. Blue predators scan over long distances for yellows and smaller blues, but stop hunting and slow down while digesting a meal. Plants remain solid barriers to predators.

Feeding

Herbivores can eat plants of equal or lower level. Higher-level plants are protected, and maximum-level plants can only be eaten by a maximum-level herbivore. After eating, herbivores slow down and must finish a digestion cooldown before eating again. Level-1 and level-2 predators can eat herbivores of equal displayed size or one pixel larger; level-3 apex predators can eat any herbivore. Predators must digest each meal before hunting or eating again. Cannibalism pauses when only three predators remain, and level-1 offspring are always protected.

Reproduction

Plants spawn naturally in open ground, and high-level plants seed much faster nearby. Maximum-level plants can produce several seedlings in one growth cycle. Herbivores must reach level 2 before mating. Eligible pairs produce three offspring when they come within a short distance with enough energy; offspring are placed in nearby open spots. Predators produce one offspring after eating two herbivores. Newborn predators appear beside their parent and remain safe from cannibalism at level 1.

Migration

At uncommon, randomized times, small groups of herbivores and predators enter from the edges of the map. Predator migration becomes more frequent when fewer than three remain, helping restore a declining or extinct population.

Leveling

Plants level up every 20 seconds they survive and cap at level 8. Higher levels attract more nearby growth, and a maximum-level plant is protected from everything except a maximum-level herbivore. Herbivores level up by eating and cap at level 5. Predators level up by eating and cap at level 3. Higher levels make moving species larger, faster, and better at seeing food.

Survival

Herbivores and predators steadily lose energy and eventually die without food. They do not die from old age unless that option is enabled in Settings. All populations share the world population limit.
